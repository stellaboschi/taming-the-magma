# Tokyo - Summer of '26
### Imaginary Journey No. 1

To the notes of The Connells - '74-'75, I introduce an imaginary journey undertaken in the summer of 2026.

Gemini:
> **Memory and time: the echo of '74-'75**  
> The lyrics of The Connells do not simply recount an era, but capture that exact intersection where youth begins to turn into memory:
> * **The weight of years gone by:** The continuous echo of a lived and now unreachable time weaves through school memories and the faded images of a youth that once seemed infinite.  
> * **The sense of "what we could have been":** Stanzas that speak of unmet expectations, of faces altered by the mirror, and of that melancholic awareness that cuts across the roaring years of youth to settle in the silence of the present.  
> * **The inexorability of time:** The music flows slowly, evoking a summer that will not return, perfect for accompanying the footsteps of an imaginary journey where past and future overlap at every street corner.
>   
> (...)  
> When a key idea—like that "what we could have been"—finds its counterpoint in a song's lyrics, the circle closes on its own. After all, both great stories and imaginary journeys thrive on these resonances: thin threads connecting the beginning and the end without the need for too much scaffolding.
